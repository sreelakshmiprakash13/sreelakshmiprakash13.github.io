AI evaluation · Lending

When should a lender trust AI to read KYC documents?

0wrong documents passed, after I found the model was 100% confident on every misread PAN
Read the case study Notebook
Question
Which AI-read PAN and Aadhaar documents can a lender safely auto-accept?
Evidence
24 specimen documents as PDF, scan and phone photo, scored field by field against ground truth. Accuracy was 98–100%, but all 4 errors came with full confidence.
Decision
Auto-accept only when free format checks pass and confidence is at least 0.95. Pilot on the upload path with human spot checks, at about ₹0.11 per document.

Fintech onboarding

Why borrowers who changed their phone number can't finish KYC

3 appswalked through end to end: Navi, KreditBee and Money View
Read the case study Prototype
Question
Where do borrowers get stuck at KYC, and what can the product fix?
Evidence
567 KYC reviews collected and 102 tagged. A PAN tied to an old mobile number is a dead end with no self-serve way out, and lenders' own FAQs confirm it.
Decision
Let borrowers move the account in the app after a live selfie and a reverse ₹1 UPI check. Never text the old number. A/B test on 7-day KYC completion with fraud guardrails.

Consumer e-commerce

Will a dishwasher work for my cooking and my kitchen?

12 of 12popular listings were replacement-only, so a wrong purchase can't be returned
Read the case study Prototype
Question
Why do first-time dishwasher buyers end up unhappy?
Evidence
My first idea, kitchen fit, showed up in about 8 of 485 reviews. Cleaning expectations drove 25 of 67 unhappy ones, so I changed the question.
Decision
A 60-second fit check on the product page: what will and won't clean well, what to prepare, which models fit, with answers passed to the installer.

How I work

The same four steps run through every case study above.

Start from evidence

Reviews, walkthroughs and test data come before opinions.

Find the root cause

Ask why until I reach something the product controls.

Decide with trade-offs

Several options, one chosen, and an honest note on what it costs.

Define how we'd know

A primary metric, guardrails and a sample size before anything is built.
