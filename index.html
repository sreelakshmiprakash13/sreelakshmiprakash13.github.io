When should a lender trust AI to read KYC documents?

Evaluated on specimen data
Problem

When instant KYC fails, borrowers upload PAN and Aadhaar photos. A misread PAN that slips through is a fraud risk for the lender. Checking every upload by hand is slow.

My role

Solo. I framed the question, designed the test, ran it, and made the call.

Decision

Auto-accept only if free format checks pass and confidence is at least 0.95.

Trade-off

Confidence alone auto-accepted all 72 but let 3 wrong PANs through. Adding format checks sends 3 more to a person and catches all 3.

Why borrowers who changed their number can't finish KYC

Proposal · prototyped
Problem

Borrowers who changed their number, or use two SIMs, are told their PAN is "already registered". There is no way out in the app, and codes go to the old number.

My role

Solo. I walked through Navi, KreditBee and Money View, analysed 102 reviews with AI-assisted tagging, and chose the fix.

Decision

Out of six options: move the account in the app with a live selfie and a ₹1 UPI payment from the borrower. Support tickets and second accounts were rejected.

Trade-off

Any recovery path can be a takeover path. A 24-hour hold, alerts and a fraud stop rule limit it.

Will a dishwasher work for my cooking and my kitchen?

Proposal · prototyped
Problem

First-time buyers can't tell if it will clean their vessels. Listings are replacement-only, so a wrong buy is stuck.

My role

Solo. I collected 485 Flipkart reviews, analysed the 67 unhappy ones with AI-assisted tagging, and changed my own idea when they disagreed.

The pivot

I started with "will it fit my kitchen?". Only 4 of the 67 unhappy reviews were about the site not being ready; 25 were about cleaning. So I changed the question.

Decision

Out of six options: an optional 60-second fit check on the product page. Rules only, no AI. Longer returns were rejected as too costly.

Trade-off

An honest "won't go in" loses some sales. Conversion is a guardrail with a stop threshold.
