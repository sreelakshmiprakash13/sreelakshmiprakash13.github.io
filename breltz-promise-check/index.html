Where the returns sit

Path to about 12% illustrative, not a forecast

The Commuter is about 30% of website revenue call, so the loop has to cover every bag, not just the hero. Each step is a guess today, and they still leave a gap to close. The 6-week pilot measures the first one, the size and fit fix. The rest are measured over two quarters. The last half point is still unexplained, and Voyager 40L is where I'd look first call.

What's at stake, rough: the margin gap is (18% − 9%) × ₹38 Cr ≈ ₹3.4 Cr a year call. Website sales are 55% × ₹38 Cr ≈ ₹21 Cr call. Going from 21.5% to 12% returns keeps roughly ₹2 Cr a year of sales from coming back, before the pickup and QC cost on each one. This assumes ₹38 Cr is before returns. A full margin bridge needs order value and product cost, which is a week 1 ask.

Which ads still pay once the returns are counted

The current rule scales ads above 3x Meta-reported ROAS and kills them below 1.8x for a week call. Meta only sees the purchase, never the return call. This view puts both numbers side by side.

Coverage: today about 40% of orders have a source call, and order IDs don't exist in Meta call. From day 1, every ad gets an ad-ID URL tag and creators get separate paid codes. The table below is the week-4 view example. While matches are few, read it by claim group, not by single ad.

Kept ROAS = Meta-reported ROAS × the share of matched orders not returned by the customer. RTO is tracked separately. Matched orders are a subset, so treat this as a proxy. Reverse pickup (₹140–180 call) and QC are not yet subtracted. A full contribution view needs order value and product cost.

Ask why at the moment of return, and save it if we can

Pooja said some laptops do fit, the buyer just "haven't used the right compartment" call. This step sits inside the Shopify returns app, before pickup. Unicommerce still processes the return. It asks the reason in plain words, tries a fix, and saves the answer with the order. Tip wording and photos are placeholders for Pooja and Farhan to confirm. If the returns app can't be customised, the same steps run on a short page linked from the return email.

What Pooja's team and this pilot see

Today a return can sit in up to three places: the Shopify returns app, Freshdesk and a Google Sheet call. Here it becomes one record with the order ID, so no one has to remember to paste it.

Why this screen is built this way
  • Pooja wants to catch a misunderstanding before the return 15:57, and today's dropdown is useless 05:14.

  • It offers a swap before a refund. That keeps the sale and avoids the ₹140–180 pickup 07:27.
